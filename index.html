How fresh is the data?

Prices (orbs, omens, essences, bones) are refreshed from poe.ninja and Craft of Exile about once an hour. Market tables for jewels and tablets come from larger trade-site scans that take hours to run, so they are snapshots with the date shown on each card.

The website cannot search the trade site for live listings, and the craft log needs a local server. For live listings and the craft log, run the tools on your own computer: see the repository README (Python 3 and a double-click on start.bat).
