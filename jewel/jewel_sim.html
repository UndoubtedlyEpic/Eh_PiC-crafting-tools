Paste a jewel from the game
Hover the jewel in Path of Exile 2 and press Ctrl+C (Ctrl+Alt+C also works), then paste it here. You can also just press Ctrl+V anywhere on this page. Fractured, desecrated and crafted lines, quality and the corrupted state are kept; the simulator continues the craft from that jewel.
Diamond rule: never more than 2 crit damage suffixes on the jewel at once, in any craft (once two are on it, no crit damage mod is offered).

Actions

Keyboard shortcuts in hand mode: C Chaos, E Exalt, A Annulment, D Divine, Z undo (only the cheap orbs, and only when you are not typing in a box). Your target mods and settings are remembered in this browser.

2 · Repeat an orb until a mod is hit (speed simulate)

until
Uses the orb you pick again and again on the jewel above, stops the moment the chosen mod is on it, and adds the whole spend to the cost. Undo takes back the whole run. Jewel mods have one tier, so the tier is the roll: 0% accepts any roll, 100% only the top value. A fractured jewel keeps its locked mod.

7 · Corruption (Vaal, double corrupt, Sacrifice)

Vaal Orb, assumed 1 in 4 each (the odds are not published): nothing happens; 1 to 3 mods are randomised; a corruption enchantment is added; a mod is added or removed ignoring the limit. After that the jewel cannot be changed, except by: the Architect's Orb (double corrupt: 50% another enchantment, 50% the jewel is destroyed), Yugul's Orb of Sacrifice (upgrades one enchantment to its stronger version and removes a random modifier) and quality. Do this after everything else.

8 · Quality (do this last)

1% per catalyst, max 20%. Only Refined catalysts work on jewels; it scales mods with that catalyst's tag.
Contempt, Ferocity and the liquids hit a random unlocked mod and replace it. Fractured mods are locked. Prices from poe2db and poe.ninja.

Time-Lost jewels

The route is the same as for the regular jewels (user); only the target mods differ. The mod pool, Ancient Liquid Emotions and catalyst tags come from the game data. The last crafted mod is Melancholy (Very Large radius) instead of Ferocity, because only one crafted modifier fits and leaving it off costs most of the price. Prices exist for the crit chance + crit damage (+ attack speed) shape only.

Before you use the simulator

This simulator keeps a record of how it is used, so the odds, costs and prices it shows can be improved and projected into the future. By ticking the box you agree to this:

  • What is saved: every simulation run and every action you take (the settings, the target mods you pick, the results, the orbs and materials used), plus any craft or sale price you choose to log.
  • What is not saved: your name, account name, email or anything else that identifies you. A random code tells one computer from another.
  • Where: checking...
  • Use: probability tables, cost and sales projections, and improving the simulator.
  • Withdraw: use "Withdraw consent" at the bottom of the page at any time; saving stops straight away.
Without agreeing, the simulator cannot be used.
