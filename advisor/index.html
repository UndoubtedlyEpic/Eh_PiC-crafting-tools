Alloys and quality: when to use them

Show the notes

Alloys (patch 0.5)

  1. What they do. An alloy works on a rare item only. It removes one random mod and adds its own fixed mod. Perfect Essences and Essence of the Breach work the same way. The table below shows what each alloy adds.
  2. Protect your good mods. The removal is random, so only use an alloy when it cannot hit a mod you want. A fractured mod is never removed. For the rest, add a throwaway mod first: an Exalted Orb with Omen of Sinistral or Dextral Exaltation puts one on the side you choose, and an unrevealed desecrated mod from a bone is a cheap throwaway too.
  3. Aim the removal. Omen of Sinistral Crystallisation limits the alloy's removal to prefixes, Omen of Dextral Crystallisation to suffixes. Two crafters use this pairing (the BigDaddy Gaming guide and Belton's wand video), although the omen's own text names only Perfect and Corrupted Essences. Check that side holds only locked mods and your throwaway.
  4. Check the slots. The alloy's mod is a prefix or a suffix (see the table) and needs a free slot on that side after the removal.
  5. Timing. Use it after every step that removes mods (Chaos Orbs, Annulments, Omen of Light), because those can remove the alloy's mod. Use it before the steps that only add mods (Exalted Orbs, desecration reveals, Greater Exaltation), so they fill the slots that are left. In the Mobalytics +7 wand guide it comes straight after the fracture and the chaos spam.
  6. Not too early. Do not spend an alloy on an unfinished base. Use it on an item where every other mod is already good.

Quality

  1. Jewellery quality comes from catalysts. There is one per tag (see the table). A catalyst replaces any other quality type and raises the values of mods with its tag. Jewels use the Refined catalysts.
  2. Maximum quality and Essence of the Breach. Quality is capped at 20%. Essence of the Breach (rings and amulets) removes a random mod and adds a guaranteed prefix: +20% to maximum quality, which lets the quality go up to 40%. You only need that prefix for a moment: add the quality while it is on the item, then take it off again.
  3. How to do it. Decide first what kind of ring or amulet you are making, such as attack, caster or mana. On the fractured item, add a junk prefix, then use Essence of the Breach so it replaces that junk prefix. (Omen of Sinistral Crystallisation limits the essence to removing a prefix, if other unlocked mods are on the item.) Add catalysts of the matching type until the quality is 40%. Then use an Orb of Annulment or a Chaos Orb to remove the +20% maximum quality prefix and free the slot.
  4. When. Do this right after the fracture, while the item is small and you can see what the Annulment or Chaos Orb can hit. Do it before the chaos spam for the second mod and before you finish the prefixes, so the slot is free again for them.
  5. Which catalyst. Match it to the type of item: Reaver for attack, Sibilant for caster, Neural for mana, and so on (see the table below).
  6. Omen of Catalysing Exaltation. The next Exalted Orb uses up all the catalyst quality and becomes more likely to add a mod with that catalyst's tag. The poe2wiki gives 5 times the weight at 20% quality and 7.5 times at 40% (unconfirmed, check it in game). If you use it, the quality is gone, so add it again afterwards.
  7. Vaal Infusers. Once an item is at its maximum quality, an Infuser adds 1% (2% about one time in six) up to 10% beyond the maximum, so 30% normally. Each use is safe at the maximum and gets riskier above it: after the quality is added there is a 5% chance of the Corrupted tag for every point above the maximum you started at (5% at 21%, 20% at 24%, 45% at 29%). Corruption only adds the tag, but then nothing can be changed any more, so use Infusers last. Source: poe2wiki, from a test of 1,734 Infusers.
  8. Armour and weapon quality. Scraps, whetstones and etchers add it. It does not affect which mods roll, so any time works. At the end is simplest.

Assumptions

Show the notes

Rules the calculator follows

  1. A Chaos Orb removes one random unlocked mod and adds a random new one. Omen of Whittling makes it remove the lowest level mod instead. Omen of Sinistral or Dextral Erasure limits the removal to a prefix or a suffix.
  2. A Fracturing Orb needs 4 or more mods and locks one random mod for good. A desecrated mod, revealed or not, counts towards the 4 but cannot be locked. Only one mod can be fractured.
  3. An Orb of Annulment removes one random unlocked mod, and that includes a desecrated mod. Omen of Light makes it remove only the desecrated mod.
  4. A bone adds a desecrated mod. On a full item a random mod is removed first and the new mod is on the same side. An item holds one desecrated mod at a time. Ancient bones guarantee mod level 40 or higher, and Gnawed bones only work up to item level 64.
  5. At the Well of Souls you pick 1 of 3 mods. They come from the normal mod pool plus the Kurgal, Ulaman and Amanamu mods. Omen of Abyssal Echoes rerolls the options once. Sinistral and Dextral Necromancy force the side. The lich omens (Sovereign, Liege, Blackblooded) guarantee one family mod, on weapons and jewellery only.
  6. Essences: Lesser, normal and Greater essences make a magic item rare with their mod. Perfect essences remove a random mod and add theirs.
  7. Minimum mod levels: Exalted, Regal and Chaos 35 (Greater) and 50 (Perfect); Transmutation and Augmentation 44 and 70.

Approximations you should know about

  1. Omen of Whittling removes the lowest level mod (the level at which the mod becomes available), with ties random. The advisor does not track the exact level of every unwanted mod, so it draws those levels from the mod pool and uses the average level of the tiers you accept for your wanted mods.
  2. A mod that shares a group with one of your targets, but is below the tier you asked for, counts as unwanted.
  3. A magic item holds 1 prefix and 1 suffix (poe2db: "Magic items can have 2 Modifiers; a Prefix and a Suffix").
  4. Desecration offers are drawn by mod weight, without repeating a mod group. At least one of the three offers is an exclusive desecrated mod when the item level is 65 or higher and the item type has one (poe2wiki). You are assumed to take a wanted mod whenever one is offered.
  5. Not in the cost calculation, only in the practice emulator: Vaal Orbs, Divine Orbs, catalysts, quality, sockets, Fluxes and Altered Collarbone. Not modelled anywhere: Omen of Putrefaction, Recombinators, Hinekora's Lock, and the omens with no market price (Coronation, Alchemy, Greater Annulment, Homogenising, Corruption). Buying a base that already has the mod you want: enter it as a mod already on the item.

Where the data comes from

  1. Mod weights: Craft of Exile. Tiers, slots and bases: Path of Building data.
  2. Prices: poe.ninja and Craft of Exile, in Exalted Orbs. You can override any price.
  3. Methods and rules: the Mobalytics guides by Lolcohol (High Damage Attack Breach Ring, Abyss Crafting) and Perra (PoE2 Crafting Guide), the TimeSaver Whittling guide, and your own corrections.
  4. The recipe from Advise me is calculated from these rules and current prices. It is not copied from a guide, so it can differ from how you would craft.
  5. All results are averages over many crafts. One craft can need more or fewer uses.
